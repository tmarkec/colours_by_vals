Behind the Brushes

Meet the Artist

Hi, I’m Val! Welcome to my creative corner. What started as a personal passion for turning everyday blank items into colorful keepsakes has grown into custom hand-painted collections.

Every piece is painted individually using quality acrylics and fine-line markers—meaning no two items are ever identical. Whether it’s a gift for a loved one or a custom treat for yourself, I put heart and detail into every stroke.
